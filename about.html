ABOUT FISCAURA

We're building books that keep themselves.

FiscAura is an AI-native accounting platform for Indian businesses. The routine work of an accounts room — reading bills, matching bank statements, keeping GST filing-ready — should run on its own. Our job is to make that happen without ever taking the human out of the loop.

The road to autonomous finance.

Not more manual software — intelligent systems that understand financial operations, automate the routine, and eventually execute with appropriate human oversight. We're at the start of that road, and we build in the open about where each step actually is.
